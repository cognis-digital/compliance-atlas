# ISO/IEC 27001:2022

- **What:** Certifiable standard for an **Information Security Management System (ISMS)**.
- **Clauses 4–10** (management system): context, leadership, planning, support, operation,
  performance evaluation, improvement — plus **internal audit & management review** (no SOC 2 analog).
- **Annex A (2022):** **93 controls** in 4 themes — Organizational (37), People (8), Physical (14),
  Technological (34). Drives the **Statement of Applicability (SoA)**.
- ~60–80% technical overlap with SOC 2; the ISMS management clauses are extra work.
