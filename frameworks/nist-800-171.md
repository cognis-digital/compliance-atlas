# NIST SP 800-171

- Protects **Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI)** in non-federal systems.
- **Rev. 2:** 110 requirements / 14 families — the basis for **CMMC Level 2**.
- **Rev. 3** (May 2024) adds supply-chain risk emphasis but **does not yet apply to CMMC**
  (Rev. 2 remains the assessment baseline).
