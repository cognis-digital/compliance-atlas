# PCI DSS 4.0 (March 2022; v4.0.1 maintenance)

- Protects cardholder data; 12 requirements / 6 goals (secure network, protect data, vuln mgmt,
  access control, monitoring, policy).
- v4.0 emphasizes **phishing-resistant MFA**, customized implementation, and continuous (not point-in-time) security.
