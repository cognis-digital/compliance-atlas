# EU AI Act

- **4 risk tiers:** unacceptable (banned), high (strict), limited (transparency), minimal (none).
- **Timeline:** prohibited practices **2 Feb 2025**; GPAI obligations **2 Aug 2025**; transparency
  **2 Aug 2026**; Annex III high-risk **2 Dec 2027**; Annex I embedded **2 Aug 2028**.
- **GPAI providers:** technical docs, training-data summary, copyright compliance; **systemic-risk**
  models add evaluations, adversarial testing, incident reporting, cybersecurity.
