# CMMC 2.0

- **Level 1** (FCI): 17 basic safeguards from FAR 52.204-21 — annual self-assessment.
- **Level 2** (CUI): **110 practices = NIST 800-171 r2**; self- or C3PAO assessment.
- **Level 3:** + 24 enhanced requirements from **NIST 800-172**.
- Rollout: since **Nov 10, 2025** new L2 contracts require self-assessment; **Nov 2026** third-party
  assessments begin.
