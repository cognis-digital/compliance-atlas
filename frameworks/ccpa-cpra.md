# CCPA / CPRA (California)

- Applies above revenue/volume thresholds (unlike GDPR's universal scope).
- **CPRA** adds a **Sensitive Personal Information (SPI)** class + the right to limit its use, and
  created the CPPA enforcement agency. Rights: know, delete, correct, opt-out of sale/share.
- Maintain a **data inventory** of what's collected, used, shared.
