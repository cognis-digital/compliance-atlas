# Crosswalk: SOC 2 ⇄ ISO 27001

- **~60–80% control overlap** (access control, risk mgmt, incident response, change mgmt).
- AICPA publishes an official TSC ⇄ ISO 27001 mapping spreadsheet.
- Doing both: a crosswalk can **cut redundant audit effort by ~40%**.
- Gap: ISO's ISMS clauses (internal audit, management review, continual improvement) have **no SOC 2
  starting point** — budget separately.
